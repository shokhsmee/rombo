# Change: add-procurement  (status: proposed — not started)

## Why
After a Loyiha is confirmed, buyers must order profile, glass, accessories and hardware from
suppliers. Today this is tracked in Excel/Telegram, so nobody sees what is ordered for which
order, what arrived, and what is late.

## What changes (draft — to be refined with the procurement team)
- **suppliers** — supplier card (TIN, contacts, payment terms, currency).
- **Purchase request** (`Xarid so'rovi`) — lines of material + qty + needed-by date, always linked
  to a Loyiha. Created manually now; generated by MRP later.
- **Purchase order** — groups request lines per supplier; statuses
  `draft → sent → partially_received → received | cancelled`.
- **Goods receipt** — what physically arrived, per PO line; partial receipts allowed.
- Loyiha status moves to `procurement` when its first PO is sent (configurable).
- Page key `procurement` already exists in the registry (planned) — flip `planned=False`.

## Open questions
- Is there a material catalogue (Akfa profile codes, glass types) to import, or do we build it here?
- Approval step on purchase requests above a limit?
- Currency handling: USD purchases, UZS sales — rate source?

## Impact
New app `apps/procurement` (depends on projects, employees). No changes to existing tables
except a nullable FK from future lines to `projects.Project`.
