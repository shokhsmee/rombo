# Change: add-procurement  (status: proposed — supersedes the 2026-10-28 stub)

Depends on: `add-accounting` (goods receipts post to the ledger). Independent of `add-kassa`
(payments to suppliers are kassa documents; this change only needs the partner balance from the ledger).

## Why
After a Loyiha is confirmed the buyer orders profile, glass and accessories from suppliers (AKFA first of
all). Today that is Excel and Telegram: nobody can answer "what was ordered for order LY-26-00042, was it
sent, what arrived, what do we owe AKFA". The process map (docs/process-map.md) puts Zakup right after
Kassa: the purchase request is "hisob-kitob lines minus stock", and the material goes to Sklad.

## What changes
Three documents, one page group **Ta'minot**, every document hanging off a Loyiha (ADR 0004):

| Document | Code | Who | Statuses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Xarid so'rovi** (purchase request) | `XS-00001` | manager / buyer | draft → submitted → approved or rejected → ordered; cancelled |
| **Buyurtma** (purchase order) | `XB-00001` | buyer | draft → **sent** → partially_received → received; cancelled |
| **Kirim** (goods receipt) | `KR-00001` | storekeeper | draft → posted; cancelled (reversed) |

The supplier's **hisob-faktura** (ESF) for goods is a block of fields on the Kirim; invoices without goods
(delivery, services) are `accounting.Invoice(kind = supplier)`.

- **Suppliers** are `clients.Client` of the new kind `supplier` (ADR 0008) with bank requisites, price-list
  currency, prepayment % and credit days. Page "Yetkazib beruvchilar".
- **Request** lines come from the Loyiha's hisob-kitob ("Hisob-kitobdan olish") or are typed; each line is
  a price-list item (or free text) + unit + quantity + needed-by date. Submit → approval by a user with the
  `purchase_approve` page (auto-approved when the submitter has it).
- **Order**: groups approved request lines per supplier; price per line (default: last price paid to that
  supplier for the item), currency and rate (USD price lists paid in so'm), VAT rate per line, expected date.
- **Send** is the step the business asked for: `POST /orders/{id}/send/` records who, when and how
  (print / email / other), moves the order to `sent`, writes the chatter line, and emails the printable
  order to the supplier when `via = email`. Sending the first order of a confirmed Loyiha moves the Loyiha
  to `procurement` (through `projects.services`).
- **Receipt**: made from an order with the remaining quantities; partial receipts allowed; posting writes
  `Dt materials / Dt vat_input  Kt ap_suppliers` to the ledger at the order price and rate, offsets any
  prepayment (`Dt ap_suppliers Kt advance_paid`) and updates the order's received quantities.
- **Supplier balance** ("qarz / avans") on the supplier card and the order comes from the ledger
  (`accounting.selectors.partner_balance`).
- Loyiha card gets a smart button "Xaridlar" (requests + orders) and the procurement status rule above.

## Out of scope (own changes later)
Returns to supplier; stock balances and material issue (Sklad); sending orders over Telegram; request lines
generated by MRP (`add-mrp` will call `services.request_create`).

## Impact
- New app `apps/procurement` (imports projects, clients, pricing, accounting). Tables:
  `procurement_purchaserequest(+line)`, `procurement_purchaseorder(+line)`, `procurement_goodsreceipt(+line)`.
- `clients.Client`: kind `supplier`; new nullable fields `bank_account, bank_name, mfo, price_currency,
  prepay_percent, credit_days`.
- Pages: `procurement` (flip `planned`), `purchase_approve`, `purchase_orders`, `goods_receipts`, `suppliers`.
- Chatter: three new tracked models in `CHATTER_PAGES`.
- Endpoints under `/api/v1/procurement/`: `requests/`, `orders/`, `receipts/`, `suppliers/` (clients of
  kind supplier with balances), actions `submit/ approve/ reject/ send/ post/ cancel/`.
- Frontend: `src/modules/procurement/` (list + form pages for the three documents, order print page),
  Loyiha smart button, nav group Ta'minot, suppliers page.
- Spec deltas: new `procurement`; `clients` (supplier kind); `projects` (auto status, smart button).
