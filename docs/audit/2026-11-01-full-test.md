## 4. Confusing for the client (wording and UX)

1. **Same word, different meaning.**
   - "O'tkazish/O'tkazilgan" = post.
   - "O'tkazma" = both a transfer kind and a journal entry.
   - "Kirim" = both a goods receipt (KR) and a stock move type, and also "Kirim / chiqim" in kassa.
   - Proposal: post → **"Tasdiqlash / Tasdiqlangan"**, journal entry → **"Provodka"**, transfer → **"Ko'chirish"**.
2. **Same thing, different word.**
   - "Loyiha" and "Buyurtma" are used for one record on one page.
   - "Sotuvchi" (form) vs "Menejer" (filter/Excel).
   - "Bekor qilindi" vs "Bekor qilingan".
   - Role-matrix names ≠ menu names ("Telefoniya" vs "Qo'ng'iroqlar", "Lidlar / voronka" vs "Voronka").
3. **Signs and colours of debt.** A supplier we owe is a negative green number in kassa and a positive red "qarz" in Procurement. Use words everywhere ("biz qarzmiz" / "mijoz qarz") with the absolute value.
4. **Currency missing next to amounts.**
   - List "Qarz" (see 10a).
   - Cash book columns on the USD kassa.
   - Partner balance in the kassa form.
   - Payment request "To'langan".
5. **English error messages leak through** ✅. Example: "Ensure this value is greater than or equal to 0." from a model validator. Fix: add Uzbek messages to `MinValueValidator`, or add a global DRF message map.
6. **Accounting codes shown to sales users.** "Mijozlar qarzi (4010)" and "tugallanmagan ishlab chiqarish (2010)" mean nothing to a manager. Show the codes only on accounting pages.
7. **Menu items that open on "Ruxsat yo'q".**
   - "Mijozlar xaritasi" needs `clients` too.
   - Telephony settings opens on a CRM endpoint.
   - CRM settings is editable on screen for view-only users, and every save fails with 403.
   - Dashboard tiles link to pages the role can't open.
8. **Statuses don't move with the work.** "Mijozga yetkazish" in production doesn't set Loyiha → Yetkazildi; staff click twice.
9. **Silent actions.**
   - "Narxlarni yangilash" re-prices an offer already sent, with no confirmation.
   - Bulk lead actions report "N updated" even when they skipped rows.
   - Merging duplicate leads counts them as *lost* and lowers the win rate.
10. **The calc lock banner** always says "ishlab chiqarish bosqichida", even in procurement, cancelled or closed.
11. **"Jami pul, so'm"** adds the USD kassa at old posting rates. Label it, or revalue at today's CBU rate.
